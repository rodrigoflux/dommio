# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences weighted equally (confirmed):

- **Latin American suppliers**: producers and exporters of fresh produce, processed foods and beverages who want international buyers but lack the sales reach to find and work them.
- **International buyers**: importers and distributors sourcing Latin American products to specific requirements (specs, quantities, packaging, destination, timelines).

Both arrive wanting to know whether DommioGlobal can run the commercial side of a cross-border deal for them, and both leave through one contact form that asks "I am a buyer / supplier".

## Product Purpose

DommioGlobal is a commercial intermediary. It matches buyer requirements with supplier offerings and manages the commercial process between them: partner search, first contact, proposals and quotations, negotiation support, closing, and follow-up for reorders. Success is a qualified inquiry from either side through the contact form.

## Positioning

One firm manages both sides of the same deal, from first contact to repeat orders, and only gets paid when the supplier gets paid. Suppliers pay no upfront fee (the commission is agreed in writing and paid on receipt of the buyer's payment). Buyers pay no fee at all.

## Operating Context

- Deals move through a fixed sequence: understand the business → find the partner → manage proposals and negotiation → keep the relationship moving.
- Logistics are agreed between supplier and buyer; DommioGlobal does not handle transport.
- Based in Querétaro, Mexico. Contact: hello@dommioglobal.com.
- Fully bilingual EN/ES; every visible string has a translation key in `JS/script.js`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS) on GitHub Pages at dommioglobal.com. No build step.
- Contact form posts through EmailJS; field names (`inquiry_type`, `from_name`, `company`, `from_email`, `phone`, `message`) and IDs must stay.
- Language switcher uses `data-translate-key` attributes and localStorage; any new text needs EN and ES strings.
- Industries served: fresh produce, processed foods, beverages.

## Brand Commitments

- Hero video (`videos/hero.mp4`, footage of a globe/map) stays as a full-screen hero (confirmed 2026-11-06).
- Original DommioGlobal blue stays (#4A90E2, darker #357ABD) (confirmed 2026-11-06).
- Name "DommioGlobal" stays.
- The scope is all of Latin America, not only Mexico (confirmed 2026-11-06). Imagery and examples must not read as one country; Querétaro is only the office address.
- Standing preference (2026-11-06): the familiar, corporate end of the spectrum. A plain white/green "category standard" build was rejected as too generic, and the bolder themed directions were passed over. Target the craft level of Faire and Tridge.

## Evidence on Hand

- None. No clients, deals, testimonials, figures or product photography exist. Never invent them; the page persuades through clarity of process and the fee model.

## Product Principles

1. Both sides read as equal parties to the same deal; neither is a footnote.
2. Specific over aspirational: say what is done at each step, not what it feels like.
3. The fee model is a reason to trust, so state it plainly and early.
4. Everything in two languages, nothing hard-coded in only one.
